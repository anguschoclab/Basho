## Part 3 — W L: JSA no-byes scheduling fidelity (NEW)

**Rule under test:** honbasho never leaves an odd man out. Every scheduled rikishi fights or forfeits (fusenshō); odd pools are rebalanced by cross-division callups cascading top-down, terminating at maezumo or an 8th bout.

### Gap analysis (verified against `src/engine/calendar/schedule.ts`)

- ✅ Makuuchi odd → juryo callup (`planJuryoCallupForMakuuchi`, W B).
- ⚠️ Juryo odd → makushita callup exists but is starved on odd days (all makushita men `busyToday`) and runs AFTER makushita scheduling — wrong order.
- ❌ Lower-division odd pools (makushita/sandanme/jonidan/jonokuchi) leave the odd man unpaired — a de-facto bye; he ends the basho with 6 of 7 bouts.
- ❌ No schedulable maezumo path for odd jonokuchi.
- ❌ No 8th-bout (hachiban-zumo) fallback.
- ✅ Mid-tournament withdrawal → fusenshō + next-day callup (W A + `oddSlots`).

### Design — `planDivisionCallups(world, basho, day)` (single top-down pass, before the division loop)

Replaces the current bottom-up, per-function callup logic. For divisions `[makuuchi, juryo, makushita, sandanme, jonidan, jonokuchi]` in order:

1. **makuuchi odd** → call up top healthy juryo rikishi (existing `planJuryoCallupForMakuuchi` logic, unchanged semantics).
2. **juryo** effective pool = roster − called-up → odd → call up top healthy makushita rikishi. Called-up juryo/makushita men are EXCLUDED from their own division's pool and JOINED to the higher division's Swiss pool; their result still credits their own `divisionRecords` (per-rikishi division — already true via `applyBoutResult`).
3. **makushita** effective pool = roster − called-up-to-juryo → odd → call up top sandanme. Cascade repeats for sandanme → jonidan → jonokuchi.
4. **jonokuchi odd** → maezumo callup (below), else 8th-bout fallback (below).
5. Output: per-division `excludeIds` (called up) + `extraPool` (called in), threaded into `scheduleDivisionDay` (new `extraPoolIds` param feeding `buildLowerDivisionSwiss`/`scheduleJuryoWithCrossover`).

Each cross-division match gets `crossDivisionCallupId` = the called-up rikishi's id (existing field; drives the schedule-card badge + narrative).

### Maezumo callup (jonokuchi odd)

- New optional `world.maezumoPool?: string[]` — ids of ephemeral unranked entrants minted at basho init (`initializeBasho`) from the newest intake (`TalentPoolMaterialization`/CandidateGenerator path), or lazily minted on first demand via `impact.collections.rikishiToAdd`.
- Maezumo entrant: `division: "jonokuchi"`, `rank: "jonokuchi"` + `isMaezumo: true` flag (new optional `Rikishi` field), bottom `rankNumber`.
- His bout counts normally for the jonokuchi man; the maezumo entrant's own result is not banzuke-relevant (guard `updateCareerRecords`/performance publish on `isMaezumo` — check `src/engine/banzuke/publish/performance.ts`).

### 8th bout (hachiban-zumo) — when no maezumo available

- On the last lower-division day (day 13), if jonokuchi remains odd after the cascade: pick a rikishi in the lowest division who has ALREADY completed 7 bouts (count `basho.matches` containing his id) — deterministic pick by banzuke ordinal.
- Mark the match `eighthBout: true` (new optional `MatchSchedule` field; copies to `BoutResult` via `enrichResult` like `crossDivisionCallupId`).
- Publish semantics: win counts toward record/kachi-koshi; loss does NOT penalize rank — consume `eighthBout` in `performance.ts` publish (verify where wins/losses feed rank movement).

### Juryo odd-day starvation fix

- Juryo callups are chosen inside `planDivisionCallups` BEFORE makushita scheduling, so a callup simply doesn't appear on the makushita card that day (the real behavior) — eliminating the `busyToday` starvation path on odd days.
- Reconcile `scheduleJuryoWithCrossover`'s `expectedSize` backfill with parity semantics: callups should satisfy parity (odd → 1), with the expected-size shortfall only for pre-tournament roster gaps — verify against actual generated roster sizes; keep behavior bounded.

### Narrative/UI

- Extend existing cross-division badge (W B) to makushita→juryo and lower-division callups on schedule cards.
- `pre_bout` beat for maezumo debut / 8th-bout callout ("asked back for an eighth"); day-recap (W K) may note an oddity day.
- Keep all display values sourced from real state.

### Tests (write first, `tests/unit/engine/matchmaking/` or `calendar/`)

- `divisionCallupCascade.test.ts` — odd pools at each level produce the correct callup pairing + exclusion threading; called-up man absent from own-division card, present exactly once on the higher card.
- `maezumoCallup.test.ts` — jonokuchi odd mints/schedules a maezumo entrant deterministically; no maezumo → 8th-bout pairing on day 13.
- `juryoOddDayCallup.test.ts` — regression: juryo callup works on odd days (the starvation bug).
- `noByesInvariant.test.ts` — simulated full basho: every active lower-division rikishi has exactly 7 bouts; every active sekitori 15 resolved-or-fusenshō; no rikishi double-booked on a day.
- 8th-bout publish: loss doesn't penalize rank movement; win counts.
- Determinism: same seed → same callup graph.
